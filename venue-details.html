Frequently Asked Questions

For Venue Owners & Bar Managers

Is my bar or restaurant already listed?
Possibly, yes. AreaBands maintains a continuously growing free directory of independently-owned bars and restaurants, including many added before the owner ever signed up. Search for your business name to check, and claim it for free if you find it.
Why is my business listed if I never signed up for anything?
A free, name-and-address-only listing doesn't require sign-up — it's part of building the most complete local directory possible. Nothing about a free listing costs anything or obligates you to anything. You're welcome to claim it, ignore it, or request removal at any time.
How much does it cost to be listed?
A basic listing (name and address) is always free. Publishing a live, self-updated show schedule is $11.97/month. Featured placement tiers are optional, separately priced upgrades, capped per zip code.
What's the difference between Center Stage, Stage Left, and Stage Right?
These are featured placement tiers controlling how prominently your live show appears. Center Stage is the single most visible placement in your zip code. Stage Left and Stage Right are smaller featured placements, limited to a fixed number of venues per zip code so the placement stays meaningful.
What if my venue doesn't have live music at all?
You're still welcome, and still free. Independently-owned bars and restaurants without live music get the same free basic listing, with an optional local-bar featured placement (Premier Listing, City Left, City Right) completely separate from the live-music tiers.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes, any paid tier can be cancelled whenever you want, and your free basic listing stays in place afterward unless you specifically ask to be removed.
How do I update my show schedule?
Once you've claimed your listing and activated the schedule feature, you manage it yourself from your own dashboard — add, edit, or remove shows any time, with changes going live immediately.
What is zip-code exclusivity and why does it matter?
Featured placement tiers are capped per zip code (for example, only one Center Stage slot per zip) so that paying for prominent placement actually means something, instead of being an unlimited tier anyone can buy into.
Do you require a long-term contract?
No long-term contract is required for standard tiers. Some top-tier exclusive placements may carry a minimum commitment specifically because of their limited, one-per-zip-code availability — full details are shown before you ever commit to anything.
How do I remove my venue from the directory entirely?
Reach out any time and it will be removed at no cost, no questions asked — there is never a penalty for choosing not to be listed.
Is this only for bars, or do restaurants qualify too?
Both. Any independently-owned bar, restaurant, or venue — whether it hosts live music or not — is welcome in the directory.
My band plays there once a month — does the venue still qualify?
Yes. There's no minimum frequency requirement. A venue that hosts live music once a month is exactly as welcome as one that hosts it every night.
